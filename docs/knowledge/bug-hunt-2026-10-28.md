# bug-hunt-2026-10-28

> Bug hunt (plan item 10, PR #73 merged 2026-10-28): audit/chaos harness tools, the three bugs fixed, what came back clean, how to trace a stall

PR #73 (squash bb2cc9d, 2026-10-28) fixed three bugs found by AI-vs-AI games:
- AI economy thrash: short of both gold and wood workers, `manageWorkers` moved one worker gold<->wood every think (61 orders/30 s). Fix: `shiftHelps(needTo, needFrom)` in src/ai/economy.js. Gather orders per AI by min 12: ~580 -> ~110; gathered +5%.
- Movement jam: Titan (r 1.6) + soldiers in a bent one-tile pass held each other forever. Fix: after 6 stuck checks in allied traffic (`mv.jams`), `u.jamT` = 2 s of ignoring allied collision/steering (src/sim/movement.js `squeezing`). Test copies the real pass from seed 6.
- Shift-queued prayer lost its reserved slot (slots only count once the order is active) -> `updatePray` re-reserves or says "full".

Tools (kept in repo): `tools/sim-audit.mjs` (invariants every 20 ticks incl. pop drift hidden by the 20-tick recount, occupancy, stalled movers, worshippers delivering nothing for 90 s, `--determinism`, `--peace=30|45|60`, `--sacred`, `--teams=2v2|ffa`); `tools/sim-chaos.mjs` (random human commands on top of AIs).
Clean: ~60 games on every invariant, determinism, delta/net already well tested. Mine/prayer slot ids are validated lazily by design (not bugs). `pop-over-cap` is legal after losing Houses.
Tracing a stall: rerun the same setup, wrap `ais[i].ai.issue` to log commands for one unit id with a short stack; a jam is confirmed by killing the other units and seeing each pass alone.
