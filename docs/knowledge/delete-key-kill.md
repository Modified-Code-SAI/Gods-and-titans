# delete-key-kill

> Delete and Backspace destroy the selected own units (PRs #48 and #54, 2026-10-27): sim `kill` command, the press-twice rule, Home for the Town Center, code map, what was left out

The owner asked (2026-10-27, one of nine parallel ideas) for the delete button to "kill" any selected unit. PR #48 (squash 1d5c53f) added the Delete key. He then answered "Do it" to "should a Mac's delete key (Backspace) do it too?", so PR #54 made Backspace do the same and moved the Town Center jump to Home. Both were squash-merged on his standing go-ahead for the batch (`auto-merge-batch-2026-10-27` (process note, not kept)): #48 as 1d5c53f, #54 as 5ec7198 (Tests and Deploy green on main both times; #54 waited about 15 minutes for the tips PR #53 to land so its Backspace tip could be fixed on top of it).

- **Sim:** `kill { units }` (COMMAND_TYPES in src/sim/commands.js, COMMANDS in src/net/sanitize.js, so the wire fingerprint changed) runs `world.killEntity(u, null)` on each own living unit: a normal death with no killer (no kills stat, xp, plunder, bounty), pop freed, loss counted, a god kept in `fallen`, orders cleaned (paid build refunded, prayer slot freed). Buildings, mines and foreign ids are skipped. Works in a Decade of Peace.
- **UI:** src/ui/delete-key.js (`deletePlan`, `DELETE_CONFIRM_ABOVE` 5, `DELETE_CONFIRM_MS` 2500) and `InputController.deleteSelected` / `pendingDelete`. Up to 5 plain units die on one press; 6 or more, or any god or Titan, shows "Press Delete again to destroy 12 units, including Zeus." (`hud.message(..., 'error')`) and needs a second press within 2.5 s while the selection is a subset of what was asked about. Esc (consumed), blur or time cancel it; key repeat, Ctrl/Alt/Cmd, chat typing and an open menu do nothing. A kill resets a pending placement or target. It takes the whole selection, not just the Tab subgroup.
- **Keys:** `Delete` and `Backspace` share one question (a press of either answers it). A Mac's delete key sends Backspace, fn + delete sends Delete. The Town Center jump Backspace used to be is `Home` (fn + left arrow on a Mac laptop); none was kept for an empty selection, since a double tap after the kill would throw the camera away. Any tip or doc that quotes the Town Center key must say Home: the tips PR (#53) had `town-key` in src/ui/hint-data.js saying Backspace, and a NAMED key list in its test.
- **Left out:** buildings (a buildings-only selection says "Delete destroys units, not buildings."; walls have D, unfinished buildings Esc), a command-card button, undo.
- **Tests and docs:** tests/sim/kill-own.test.js (7), tests/ui/input-delete.test.js (11), the `good` list in tests/net/hardening.test.js, the How to Play test; DESIGN.md 6.2 and 9, README, How to Play, notes/decisions.md.

**Why:** no undo, so a big army or a god gets one extra press; a few plain units should not.
**How to apply:** a building-delete change starts in deleteSelected; a new command type also needs the hardening `good` entry. The follow-up push route: `force-push-denied-use-ours-merge` (process note, not kept). Merge procedure: `pr-merge-workflow` (process note, not kept).
