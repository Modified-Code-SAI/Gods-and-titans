# rich-centaur-plan

> Greek Centaur and Celtic Dullahan rich centaurs (person on a horse, one skin on humanoid + horse bones, PR #44, creature pass 8): code map, bones, pose, values, costs, checks

Part 18 of "Yes finish them next" (2026-10-24). Draft PR #44 stacked on #43 (branch `claude/creatures-centaurs-20tcd3`). Greek Centaur (creature, scale 1.25: buckskin horse with the team's saddle cloth, archer bare to the waist, bow, quiver, baldric of the team's cloth) and Celtic Dullahan (creature, scale 2: headless rider in black iron on a black steed, his head held up by its hair in the left hand, whip of a spine). A plan of its own in the dispatch: `richSupported` asks `centaurSupported` (body `centaur`, human head or none, one head, no headgear, extras only beard/long_hair/cape/armor/eye_glow/tail), built by the humanoid template path in rich-humanoid.js (`centaur` flag, `centaurFrame`, `raiseDims`, `addCentaurBones` before `makeBinds`, `settle` after `kit.build`); template `kind: 'centaur'`, `rich.plan = 'centaur'`, `rich.ix = spec.ix`.

**Code** (src/render/models): `rich-centaur.js` (HORSE build = quadruped equine build made broader/deeper with the barrel ending under the person; `centaurFrame`, `raiseDims`, `addCentaurBones` = 19 horse bones after the humanoid's 23: hips, back, withers, tail1-4, `F1u/F1l/F1p`, `F-1..`, `B1..`, `B-1..`; pelvis re-parented to the withers; `addPalmBone`; `addHorse(E,o)`; `settle`), `rich-centaur-outfits.js` (`CENTAUR_OUTFITS` greek/celtic laid over the pantheon outfit by `outfitFor(pan,'centaur')`; the `legs` layer is the horse), `rich-centaur-anim.js` (`applyCentaur`, called from `applyCreature` case `centaur`), `rich-dullahan.js` (`severedHead`, `spineWhip`). Hooks: `gear(ctx,item)` may return `{head:true,pivot:true}` (lantern) or `{build:true,spine:true}` (whip); `style.pivot` builds the piece without a hand frame on bone `spec.ix.carry` (child of handL at the palm), which the pose turns upright. `addHeadAndNeck` has a headless branch (stump with a raw cut cap). `PROFILES.centaur` = the humanoid values the old inline low-poly code used; `richDims` scales the rich person 1.12x (`CENTAUR_PERSON`). unit-anim.js `quadPose` writes a centaur's arm keys when first seen dead. `layout`/`BUILDS` exported from rich-quadruped.js, `strike` from rich-anim.js.

**Pose**: horse as applyQuadruped (root bob, pitch bent through hips/back/withers, legs from `gFL..kBR`, 4-bone tail wave); rider as applyRich (lean/twist/arms from `aRz..tRx`, neck/head counter-lean); pelvis turned back by -0.85 pitch and -0.7 yaw so the chest stays within ~0.09 rad at the gallop (pelvis 0.01-0.02 when the front tips 0.07-0.11).

**Costs and checks**: 7536 (Centaur) and 8842 (Dullahan) triangles, creature budget 10500; height 1.72 and 2.40 (1.07x and 1.14x low-poly). tests/render/rich-centaurs.test.js (15 tests); rich-models.test.js Greek and Celtic gates no longer list the centaur and Dullahan as low-poly. Checksums vs the nagas branch: all ten pantheons identical, rich and low-poly, only the two new units differ. Traps: [rich-centaur-look-traps](rich-centaur-look-traps.md). Overview: [rich-creatures-plans](rich-creatures-plans.md).
