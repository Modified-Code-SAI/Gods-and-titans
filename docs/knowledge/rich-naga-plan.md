# rich-naga-plan

> Hindu Naga, Nuwa and Quetzalcoatl rich nagas on the humanoid rig with a nine-bone serpent tail (PR #43, creature pass 7): files, values, costs, checks

Part 17 of "Yes finish them next" (2026-10-24). Draft PR #43 stacked on #42 (branch `claude/creatures-nagas-20tcd3`). Hindu Naga (creature, scale 1.4, cobra head and hood, kirita, spear), Chinese Nuwa (god, 1.6, tiara, long hair, ribbon, staff and stone), Aztec Quetzalcoatl (god, 1.7, plume crown, wings, staff). Not a plan of its own, like [rich-spirit-plan](rich-spirit-plan.md): body `naga` is in `RICH_BODIES`; dims from `PROFILES.naga` (unit-bodies.js, values the old inline override had); template `kind: 'naga'`.

**Code** (src/render/models): `rich-naga.js` has the tail: `TAIL` keys (14, an S on the ground ending in a curled tip), `nagaBones` (9 joints, keys 2,4,6..12), `addNagaBinds`, `nagaTail(E,o)` (one loft, 40 stations x `kit.sides + 2`, `ref` BACK = [-.707,.707,0], ground clamp), `scalesOf` (diamonds, back tone, cream belly in plates, optional lapped feathers), `dorsalDiamonds`, `nagaBelly`, bands, `tailFan`, `nagaWaist` (belt over the torso-to-tail seam), `onTail`. `rich-naga-head.js` `cobraHead` = serpent skull from rich-reptile-heads.js on the head bone plus a hood loft; `BEAST_HEADS` took `serpent`. Dresses live in rich-outfits-hindu/-chinese/-aztec.js `legs` layers (`nagaTail` where legs would go). Kit: `humanoidSpec(d,{tail,tailStyle})`, `spec.tail = {first,count,style}`. Anim: `poseTail` style `serpent` in rich-anim.js, yaw-only wave `sin(wavePhase - 0.8 i + seed)(0.05+0.03 i) sweep`, sweep .45 still to 1.0 moving.

**Costs and checks**: 5562 (Naga), 8726 (Nuwa), 8988 (Quetzalcoatl) triangles; god budget 9000 so Quetzalcoatl has 12 left (wings 1.5k, staff 1.05k, tail 1.6k). tests/render/rich-nagas.test.js (15 tests); rich-models.test.js gates no longer list the three as low-poly. Checksums vs the spirits branch: all ten pantheons identical, rich and low-poly, only the three nagas new. Traps and framing: [rich-naga-look-traps](rich-naga-look-traps.md). Overview: [rich-creatures-plans](rich-creatures-plans.md).
