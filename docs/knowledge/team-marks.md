# team-marks

> Minimap pings and team markers (PR #52, 2026-10-27 batch): gestures, the teammates-only `mark` message, limits, code map and decisions

The owner asked (2026-10-27, one of nine parallel ideas, `parallel-ideas-batch-2026-10-27` (process note, not kept)) for a mouse click to ping the minimap and place markers for teammates. PR #52 "Teams: minimap pings and team markers", squash-merged as 2b5c75e on his standing go-ahead (`auto-merge-batch-2026-10-27` (process note, not kept)); `test` green on the head, no review threads when it went, Tests and Deploy green on main.

- **Wire:** a mark is a signal, not world state, so it is its own `mark` message and not a snapshot field (snapshots go to everyone). The client sends `{t:'mark', kind, x, y}`; `HostSession` takes it through `CLIENT_LIMITS.mark` (`MARK_RATE` 1.5/s, burst 5), `sanitizeMark` and `relayMark(from, mark)`, which sends `{t, from, kind, x, y}` only to the open connections of the sender's teammates (`from` is the connection's own index) and to the host's screen if it is on that team. The sender shows a local echo (`SessionBase.emitMark`). Clients re-check `from` (integer, not self, same team via the setup) and flood-limit a host (`CLIENT_MARK_LIMIT` 8/20). `MARK_KINDS` is in the wire fingerprint.
- **Gestures:** middle-click (any mode; a press under 350 ms that moved under 5 px, else it is the old camera drag) and Alt+click (only with no order or placement pending, because Alt held shows all health bars and a click that aims an order must stay one), on the map or the minimap. Five marker buttons beside the minimap arm `{kind:'target', order:'mark', mark}` and reuse Esc, right-click and Shift handling; a refused mark keeps the button armed.
- **Numbers (src/data/marks.js):** ping lives 6 s, attack/defend/help/gather 12 s; 3 marks per player (oldest retired), a typed marker replaces its sender's earlier one of that kind, 16 per screen; send pace `MARK_SEND_RATE` 1/s burst 4, a little under the host's bucket so a mark the sender saw is never silently dropped.
- **Code map:** src/game/team-marks.js (store), src/ui/hud-marks.js (`MarksLayer` DOM pins via `renderer.worldToScreen`, edge chips with arrows that `camera.centerOn`, `MarkPalette`, `teammateCount`), `Minimap.drawMarks`, `InputController` (`markSpot`, `pingAt`, `armMark`, `midDown`), `Game.sendMark` / `onMark`, sfx `ping` / `ping_urgent`, How to Play group "Team signals".
- **Decisions:** only a human teammate sees a mark (no teammate: the sender sees their own and the start hint is skipped; an AI has no screen). Left alone: overlapping edge chips when many marks lie one way, hotkeys for markers, marks for late joiners.
- **Tests:** tests/net/marks.test.js (loopback sessions; other teams get nothing), team-marks, hud-marks, input-marks. The fake DOM has no `getComputedStyle`, so `MarksLayer.playArea` falls back to 11 px per em. How the browser check was run: [two-tab-browser-check](two-tab-browser-check.md).

**Why:** teammates need to say "here" without typing, and the other team must never learn where an attack is called.
**How to apply:** a new mark kind is one entry in `MARKS` plus a how-to line; a new gesture goes in `InputController.onMouseDown` and `Minimap` together. Merge steps: `pr-merge-workflow` (process note, not kept).
