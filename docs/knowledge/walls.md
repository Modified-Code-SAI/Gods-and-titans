# walls

> Wooden and stone walls (PR #18 and review follow-up #19, both merged 2026-10-21): what the owner asked, the rules as built, tuning knobs, open follow-ups

2026-10-21: the owner asked that Worshippers build walls that start as wood and upgrade to stone, and that a tower built on or in line with a wall becomes part of it. PR #18 (branch claude/project-thread-jqicia) was opened as a draft after #15, #16 and #17 landed and squash-merged as 9cfb20e on his first "Merge it." about 7 minutes later (Tests and Deploy green, see `pr-merge-workflow` (process note, not kept)); he had not reported hand-testing yet. Spec: DESIGN.md 5.3 (also 4.3, 5.5, 5.8, 6.2, 6.3, 8, 9, 10); How to Play and the README mention walls. Code map: [wall-code-map](wall-code-map.md).

- Segments are ordinary 1x1 buildings (`wall: true`): `wooden_wall` 12 wood, 350 hp, armor 1, 6 s; `stone_wall` 1000 hp, armor 6, cost = whole price 20 gold + 22 wood. Stone comes only from the per-segment upgrade (20 gold + 10 wood, 12 s, no worker, no tier needed), which swaps the entity's type in place. A tower may be raised over own segments (they go, full refund) and joins the wall.
- Players drag out a line (up to 48 tiles, 8 directions, paid at once, sites appear at once); workers share stretches. U upgrades and D dismantles (50% refund) a whole selection of up to 160 segments.
- Walls block every ground unit, own included, and give no XP; they do not count for defeat, pop or stats. Idle soldiers and towers ignore them; a blocked attacker breaks one (`findBreach`), archers included when they cannot get in range any other way (intended, slow). 6 warriors break wood in about 6 s and stone in about 23 s. The AI builds none and ignores them in scans, but its armies break through. The Decade of Peace holds through `canHarm` ([decade-of-peace](decade-of-peace.md)).
- Knobs: config.js MAX_WALL_LINE, WALL_HELP_RANGE, WALL_PLACE_RANGE, WALL_REMOVE_REFUND, MAX_WALL_SELECTION, BREACH_RANGE; strength and costs in buildings.js.
- Open for the owner: no gates (dismantle a segment for a gap), no Town-tier gate on stone, the AI builds no walls, and whether 24 tiles for `WALL_PLACE_RANGE` feels right (my first guess; he has not reported hand-testing).

Review follow-up, PR #19 (squash-merged 2026-10-21 as 396c086; opened after the owner answered "Looks good." to a decision card whose recommended option was fixes plus a range limit): the bots found seven things after #18 merged. Fixed: a defeated player's wall segments now die with them (`defeatPlayer`), `build` only takes `BUILD_MENU` types (`buildableDef`, so no direct Stone Wall), wall lines go up only within `WALL_PLACE_RANGE` = 24 tiles of an ordered worker (first-pass number; the sim does not know the fog), the crowd panel rebuilds when walls turn to stone, an off-map release orders nothing, `s.cut` in surround.js resets once a spot is reachable. Kept: ranged units breaking a wall that blocks their only way in; the docs were corrected.

**Why:** later work on buildings, pathing, the AI, the HUD or the minimap must know walls are buildings that do not count as buildings.
**How to apply:** for gates or AI walls start from DESIGN.md 5.3 and `issueBuildLine`; stack and decisions in [mythic-war-game](mythic-war-game.md). A test that counts a defeated player's walls must read them while their Town Center still stands (see tests/ai/ai-walls.test.js).
